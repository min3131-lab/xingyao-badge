# 星瑶拓展页素材

背景使用内置 imagegen 生成，未使用官方图标或官方背景。原始输出已复制为 `星瑶-星空背景.png`，源输出文件为 `exec-f4d13119-223e-46ac-b709-b1baabe48059.png`。

三个图标（对话、设置、息屏）由 `scripts/build_badge_desktop.py` 使用基本矢量形状绘制；与背景一起预转换为原生 466×466 RGB565 背景和 112×112 RGB565A8 图标。背景只进行尺寸及像素格式转换，不修改插画内容。

参考资料：开发前查阅了微雪 GitHub 官方 Brookesia 桌面布局（`waveshareteam/ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.75`，提交 `e4344e70c2fa78a13e8a06566507f1ba8af6672a`）；仅借鉴圆屏安全范围和大图标交互。运行时复用项目已有 LVGL 控件、手势和动画 API，不引入额外桌面框架。

本版按用户要求没有角色应用、返回角色按钮或虚构分页；徽章为主界面，拓展页可下滑或右滑收起。语音入口命名为“对话”。徽章名称为“星瑶”。

## 背景精确提示词（内置工具）

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original wallpaper for a 466x466 round AMOLED anime character badge launcher. Generate one square background illustration ONLY, no device, no UI mockup, no icons, no text, no person. It should feel like the personal little world of an anime girl with lavender hair, pink tips, a crystal star hair clip and a dark jacket with tiny icy cyan accents. Elegant restrained anime art direction, deep midnight violet and navy with subtle lavender/pink luminous wisps along the extreme lower left and upper right edges, a few delicate tiny four-point glints and faraway stars. Mostly very dark uncluttered center with ample quiet space for four app icons and white Chinese captions to be overlaid later. Atmospheric, soft, clean gradients with very fine grain to avoid banding; gentle low brightness, dreamy and intimate, high quality anime collectible badge aesthetic. No landscapes, no buildings, no planets, no crescent moons, no concentric circles, no frames, no borders, no heavy dense starfield, no large bright objects. All corners are expendable because hardware will crop square to circle. Entire image full bleed square.
